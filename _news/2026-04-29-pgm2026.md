The 13th International Conference on Probabilistic Graphical Models (**PGM 2026**) will be held in **Valencia**, Spain, from **September 9–11, 2026**. 

I am part of the local organizing committee, and we are looking forward to welcoming the community to Valencia.

Key dates:
*   **Abstract Submission:** May 15, 2026
*   **Paper Submission:** May 23, 2026
*   **Conference:** September 9-11, 2026

For more information, visit the [official conference website](https://www.uv.es/pgm2026/).
